## Why FDE Framing Is Credible (not invented)

FDE roles at AI-native companies (Palantir-style, and the AI infra/vertical-AI companies that
dominate this queue) are looking for: technically fluent people who can sit inside a customer's
environment, understand their data/systems, build/configure a working solution fast, and act as
the translation layer between customer reality and product engineering. This is NOT the same bar
as "senior backend engineer" — it is "technical, customer-embedded, builds things that work."

Barron's real, defensible technical surface area:
- Built and operated a full-stack **AI-powered self-serve onboarding + automation platform**
  (PicoPilot AI) — worked directly with Data Science/Engineering on ML-driven workflows, not just
  requirements docs
- Owns a **multi-system attribution and data pipeline** (Segment, Rockerbox, BigQuery, SQL) that
  unifies 5+ ad platforms into one measurement layer — this is real systems integration work
- Built **agentic bid-optimization and creative-rotation automation** — configuring/operating
  automated decisioning systems in production, with real-time data feedback loops
- **SQL** across every role listed (Alibaba, Next2Market) for building dashboards, cohort models,
  and forecasting — this is genuine hands-on data work, not "worked with data teams"
- Deployed **payment/API integrations** (Alipay B2B wallet across 5M+ accounts) — cross-border
  compliance + technical integration, not just a business deal
- History of being the **embedded operator inside a customer's stack** — WeWork Labs advisory
  work was literally sitting inside 18+ portfolio companies' systems (CRM, data pipelines) to fix
  and rebuild their GTM infrastructure — this is structurally identical to what an FDE does,
  just for growth/ops systems instead of a vertical AI product
- Built and shipped **automation that reduced manual process load by 40%** (WeWork GTM cadence)
  — a direct analog to an FDE's "eliminate the customer's manual workflow" mandate

## What NOT to claim (hard boundaries)

- NEVER claim a Computer Science degree, a SWE title, or "wrote production code in [language]"
  unless a specific scripting/SQL claim is already true per the experience bank
- NEVER invent a specific programming language proficiency (Python, Java, etc.) that isn't
  grounded in real experience bank content. SQL is real and provable — claim SQL specifically,
  not "full-stack development"
- NEVER claim to have built or shipped a customer-facing software product as an engineer
- The honest positioning is: **technical operator / implementation & systems generalist who has
  built and run production automation and data systems, and has direct experience being embedded
  in customer/portfolio environments** — not "software engineer pivoting into FDE"

## Executive Summary Pattern for FDE Roles

Sentence 1: Position as a technical/systems operator with direct customer-embedded delivery
experience — NOT "growth marketer," lead with "technical implementation" / "systems" / "embedded
operator" language.
Sentence 2-3: PicoPilot AI (ML-driven system, cross-functional with Eng/DS) + WeWork Labs
portfolio embedding (0→1 systems builds across 18+ companies) as the two strongest FDE-relevant
proof points — these should usually anchor the summary regardless of which company they're
chronologically from.
Sentence 4: Bridge sentence citing SQL/data-pipeline/API-integration fluency across the full
career arc.
Sentence 5: Name the target company's product/mission specifically, and state directly why an
embedded technical-implementation background fits their FDE motion.
